01 / UNDERSTAND

What is SDG 12?

SDG 12 aims to ensure sustainable consumption and production patterns – doing more and better with less.

OUR CHALLENGE

We consume more than the planet can give.

Our economy mostly follows a linear "take – make – throw away" model. Around one third of all food produced is lost or wasted, fast fashion fills landfills with clothes worn only a few times, and single-use plastics pollute rivers and oceans.

SDG 12 calls for a circular economy: reducing waste, producing sustainably, keeping materials in use through repair, reuse and recycling, and helping people make informed, sustainable choices.

LESS WASTE · MORE VALUE

Key SDG 12 targets

12.1

Sustainable consumption plans

All countries implement the 10-Year Framework of Programmes on sustainable consumption and production.

12.2

Natural resources

By 2030, achieve sustainable management and efficient use of natural resources.

12.3

Halve food waste

By 2030, halve per capita global food waste at retail and consumer levels and reduce food losses.

12.4

Chemicals & waste

Environmentally sound management of chemicals and all wastes, reducing their release to air, water and soil.

12.5

Reduce, reuse, recycle

By 2030, substantially reduce waste generation through prevention, reduction, recycling and reuse.

12.6

Corporate reporting

Encourage companies to adopt sustainable practices and include sustainability information in their reports.

12.7

Public procurement

Promote sustainable public procurement practices in line with national policies.

12.8

Awareness for all

By 2030, people everywhere have the information and awareness for sustainable lifestyles.

12.b

Sustainable tourism

Monitor the impacts of tourism that creates jobs and promotes local culture and products.

Seven actions you can take today

Buy less

Ask yourself if you really need it before you buy – the most sustainable product is the one you don't buy.

Buy quality

Choose durable products that last for years instead of cheap items that break quickly.

Compost food waste

Plan meals, use leftovers and compost what is left to return nutrients to the soil.

Choose sustainable brands

Support companies that are transparent about materials, workers' rights and their environmental impact.

Repair, don't replace

Fix clothes, electronics and furniture – or visit a local repair café.

Buy second-hand

Thrift shops, swaps and online marketplaces give products a second life.

Reduce plastic

Carry a reusable bottle, bag and cup, and avoid single-use packaging.
